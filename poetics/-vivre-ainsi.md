# To live thus and from then on

To live thus and from then on,

Is to be an Exalted one, staggering under the lushness of life's howls…

Is to be expressed through laws,

Of the most Intensifying and Complexifying from which one may exult

**Thus are the tales**

**of these instants**

Dream of the journeys of these perspectives and destinations,

For from the abundance of their disparate wanderings,

Traces that disorient fade away.

Dream of the vehemences of this wealth and vitality,

For in the discordance of these tales,

**There is only the Extreme!**

**In the EARTH lies Exuberance…**

**An immortal blaze of passions and potentials**

**under jubilations and euphorias**

of Folkloric blizzards and Technological tidal waves ...

**Discording Tales**

**Shine, Illuminate and Enluminé!**

**The Unmemoried, the Invisible, and the Inexplicable…**

Magic births Image: Animated, Manifest, Imaginable.
