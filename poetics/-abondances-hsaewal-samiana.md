# The 4 Abundances of Hsæwâl and Samianâ

hôMôh, when his most ardent and sparkling flames burned his coldest and deadliest embrace, separated from Hael two astral Rildées, HSÆWÂL (Vigor / Tolerance / Pleasure / Fidelity) she inheriting his vigorous and adult constitution, then SAMIANÂ (Empathy / Generosity / Poetry / Pity) she enriching herself with his mature and inspired empathy.

- Before Ô welcomed Hael, they endured with her the fevered burns and deadly frosts of their tormentors. Hsæwâl feasted on insatiable famine upon her juicy and sweet Physrils ( Growing Forms / Plants ) caused by her harvests scorched when Môh was aflame. Samianâ, dependent on her herds of Zoosrils ( Moving Forms (of them- selves ) / Animals), submitted to the frozen pastures of Môh when refrigerated.
- Surging through Môh from the meanders of a whirlwind just like Hael, both emerged side by side with her, launching into their unfathomable quests, the first moons to roam Féos. Before leaving it, Hsæwâl sowed her robust seeds of herbs, flowers and berries in the golden hides of Samianâ's comforting herds by their sheen, which she offered to Hael to cover her so all might walk without hunger or troubles, guided by the four tutelary major Rildées born from them, these PAUK PEYTSK ( "Nurseling" Lutins ) of localities who help all in work without ever tiring, though habitually trampling each other just like the Yômmes of our times.
- One dawn, they were inspired by the most harmonious symphony of the Siôiz of the HISM guiding them out of their typical discordance through their divergent interests. Hôgmadz picked and sorted the most vigor- ous seeds of Hael be- yond the fields of Hôghemn, who sowed and cultivated them in the most fertile lands of Hael enclosed within the terri- tories of Mêmhwal, who hunted and captured the most vigorous beasts of Hael far from the fresh pastures of Mêmmelg, who fed and raised them thanks to the labors of all his brothers in order to birth the Yômmes them- selves, before letting them graze where they wished.
- Up there, it is after each 18th Dusk that Hsæwâl the fourth finally sates her famine before her next harvest burns again
- And after each 24th Dawn, Samianâ the fifth calms her beasts at milking to drink of her addiction, before succumbing to the arcana of imminent weaning.
Mêmmelg ( Pastoralism ) milks in Soliarque his powerful beasts and shears in Naîstreï his majes- tuous cattle, though he is often repelled by Shdrazd for grazing upon his lands;

Mêmhwal ( Hunting & Fishing ) hunts in Albaîme the vigorous mastodons, and fishes in Frimeï those proudly swimming upstream;

Hôghemn ( Agriculture ) irrigates in Esveildor his graceful fields of barley through ingenuity, and sows his seeds in Auneï;

And finally Hôgmadz ( Gathering ) collects in Luisarie his secret berries, and harvests in Fâneï his inac- cessible honeys.
