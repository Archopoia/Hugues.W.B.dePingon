Mission & Vision

After years of study, questioning, and writing, I worked on what I think of as a philosophical fiction - developing a fictional world to explore ideas through visual design, cultures, and storytelling. I wrote it first as a book, illustrated it in part, and am now turning it narratively into a trailer with a team - to reach as many people as possible through aesthetics.

Aesthetics is what I keep returning to. I learn traditional arts and traditional architecture as I go, while remaining attentive to how people perceive, embody, and inhabit culture. I wish to make beautiful, meaningful work that inspires people to act in life.
